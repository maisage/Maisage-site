How to read what you find

🚨
Red flags
• Repeated serious deficiencies
• Ongoing enforcement actions
• Major staffing gaps
• Neglect patterns in complaints
⚠️
Yellow flags
• Minor repeated issues
• Borderline but improving staffing
• Recent ownership change
• Moderate complaint volume
✅
Positive signs
• Deficiencies trending down
• Stable, long-tenured ownership
• Staffing at/above state avg
• Issues corrected quickly

Numbers tell you where to ask harder questions

A facility with some past issues but clear improvement can be very different from one with ongoing, unresolved problems. Ownership and administrator stability often correlate with culture. Staffing numbers are a clue, not a guarantee — you still need to visit and observe.
